Straight answers other buyers skip

If a company tells you "no lawyers, no court, the other owner never has to know," ask them about these.

Selling your share doesn't take you off the mortgage

If you signed the loan as a borrower, you stay on it until the loan is paid off or refinanced. Ask any buyer how and when that will happen. We put it in writing.

Married co-owners usually can't sell alone

Many states let spouses own as "tenants by the entirety." Then one spouse generally can't sell a share without the other, until a divorce changes how title is held. We check before we offer.

The other owner will find out

Our deed is recorded in the public land records and we become their co-owner. We contact them respectfully about a buyout or a sale. You don't have to be part of that.

Watch for offers that drop after the title search

Questions

Can I sell my share without the other owner's permission?

Usually, yes. In most states an owner who holds title as a tenant in common or joint tenant can sell their own share without the other owners signing. The big exception is a married couple who own as tenants by the entirety; one spouse generally can't sell alone. A divorce court order, an LLC agreement or a right of first refusal can also limit a sale. We check how title is held before we offer.

If I sell my share, am I off the mortgage?

No. Selling your ownership share doesn't remove you from a loan you signed as a borrower. Only a payoff, a refinance or the lender's release does that. Tell us if you're on the loan and we'll build the plan around it.

Will the other owner find out?

Yes, eventually. Our deed is recorded in the public land records and we become their co-owner. We contact them respectfully to work out a buyout or a sale. You don't have to be involved.

Why would you pay less than half the value for a half share?

Because a share isn't the whole house. We take on the loans, the cost and time of a possible partition lawsuit, holding costs and the risk the other owner fights. Our offer starts from your share of the real equity, after loans and liens, minus those costs, and we show you that math in writing.

Why do some companies' offers drop after the title search?

Quick offers are often based on what the seller remembers about value and loans. When the title search finds a second mortgage, a home equity line, a judgment or a lien, the number gets cut. We pull those records before we give you a number.

Do you buy in my state?

Yes, in all 50 states, closing through a title company or closing attorney licensed where the property is. You don't have to travel.

Is a partition lawsuit better than selling my share?

Sometimes. With a lot of equity, money for an attorney and 9 to 18 months to wait, a partition sale usually pays more. Selling your share is faster, costs you nothing out of pocket and ends your involvement. The calculator above shows both.
