What usually happens next in Maryland

Every family’s situation is different, but most inherited houses in Maryland go through the same few steps.

  1. Someone opens the estate

    A family member or the person named in the will files with the Register of Wills in the county where your loved one lived. That person becomes the personal representative.

  2. Small estate or regular estate

    If the probate assets are worth $50,000 or less ($100,000 or less when a surviving spouse is the only heir), Maryland has a simpler small estate process. Larger estates go through regular administration.

  3. The personal representative can sell

    Once the Register of Wills issues Letters of Administration, the personal representative can usually sell the house without a court order, unless the will or the court limits that. Heirs can’t sign a deed on their own.

  4. Bills first, then each heir’s share

    Keep the house insured and the property taxes paid. Sale proceeds pay valid debts and estate costs first, and the rest goes to the heirs. Close family members are usually exempt from Maryland’s inheritance tax.

Nobody has opened the estate yet? That’s very common, and it doesn’t stop you from asking questions. The filing itself is handled by the family, often with an estate attorney. For more detail, read our full guide: How to sell an inherited house in Maryland.

Your options, honestly

Selling to us isn’t always the right answer. Here’s how we’d think about it if it were our family.

IF SOMEONE WANTS IT

Keep the house

If a family member wants to live there or rent it out and can carry the taxes, insurance and upkeep, keeping it can make sense. Siblings can sometimes buy out the others’ shares.

IF IT’S IN GOOD SHAPE

List with an agent

If the house is in good condition, the heirs agree and there’s time for showings, listing it usually brings the highest price. We’ll tell you if that’s your situation.

IF IT’S COMPLICATED

Sell it as-is to us

Repairs, a lifetime of belongings, heirs in different states, a stalled estate or a family that just wants it handled. We buy houses as they are and work with the estate’s attorney and title company.
