We buy houses during and after a divorce

Pages of Purpose LLC is a home buyer that specializes in houses that have to be sold, or bought out, because of a divorce or separation. We make one written offer both spouses and both attorneys can see, buy the house as-is with no showings, and time the closing to the settlement agreement or court order. Based in Maryland, buying in all 50 states.

By Dustin Ray, Founder of Pages of Purpose LLC. Pages of Purpose is a real estate company, not a law firm, and nothing on this page is legal advice. Speak with an attorney licensed in the state where the property is located.

🙋 Who calls us

  • 💍 Spouses who agree the house has to go but can't face months of showings and negotiating together.
  • 🏠 The spouse who moved out and is still paying for a house they can't use or sell alone.
  • 📄 People with a court order or settlement agreement that says "sell the house" with a deadline.
  • ⚖️ Divorce attorneys and mediators who need a fast, transparent number to settle the property split.

🔑 What we actually do

📄 One offer, in writing, to both sides

Everyone sees the same number and the same terms, which takes the "who's hiding what" fight out of the sale.

🏚️ As-is, no showings

No repairs, no staging, no strangers walking through while you're still living apart under one roof.

🗓️ Closing timed to the case

Before the divorce is final, at the decree, or after, whatever the agreement or court order says. The title company splits the proceeds the way the order directs.

If one spouse wants to keep the house, an independent as-is number helps set a fair buyout price, and we'll say so if a buyout makes more sense than selling.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Can we sell our house before the divorce is final?

Usually yes, if both spouses sign or a court order allows it. Many couples sell first and divide the proceeds under the settlement agreement. Pages of Purpose LLC buys houses before, during and after divorce in all 50 states.

Can one spouse sell the house without the other?

Generally not if both are on the deed, and in many states not even when only one is, because of marital or homestead rights. A court can order a sale if one spouse refuses.

How are the proceeds split?

The title company pays off the mortgage and costs, then splits what is left exactly as the settlement agreement or court order says. We never decide that; the order does.

Should one of us just buy the other out?

Sometimes that's the better answer. If one spouse can refinance and wants to stay, a buyout keeps the house in the family. Our as-is number can help you both agree on a fair buyout price.

Are you lawyers?

No. Pages of Purpose LLC is a real estate company, not a law firm, and nothing we say is legal advice. Each spouse should have their own family-law attorney.

Which states do you buy in?

All 50. We are based in Maryland and have divorce guides for North Carolina and Maryland, plus a national guide, with more added every week.
