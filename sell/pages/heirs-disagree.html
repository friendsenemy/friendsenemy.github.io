We buy inherited houses when the heirs can't agree

Pages of Purpose LLC is a home buyer that specializes in inherited houses stuck between family members: one sibling wants to sell and another won't, nobody opened probate, an heir can't be found, or the person living in the house won't leave. We do the heir and title research ourselves and make offers that work for one heir or for all of them. Based in Maryland, buying in all 50 states.

By Dustin Ray, Founder of Pages of Purpose LLC. Pages of Purpose is a real estate company, not a law firm, and nothing on this page is legal advice. Speak with an attorney licensed in the state where the property is located.

🙋 Who calls us

  • 👫 The heir who wants out and is tired of paying taxes and insurance on a house a brother or sister won't sell.
  • 📋 The executor or administrator caught between beneficiaries who each want something different.
  • 🏠 The heir living in the house who would like to stay or buy the others out but can't get the numbers to work.
  • 🧓 Families where nobody opened probate years after a parent or grandparent died, so the deed is still in a dead person's name.

🔑 What we actually do

🔎 Find every heir

We trace who inherited under the will or the state's intestacy rules, including heirs of heirs, and use genealogical and skip-trace research to find people the family lost touch with.

📜 Map the title

Whose name is on the deed, whether probate was opened, what is owed in taxes and liens, and what it will take to give a buyer clean title.

🤝 Offer options, not ultimatums

A cash offer for the whole house, an offer for one heir's share, or a partnership offer. One heir can often sell their interest even when the others won't, and that alone frequently ends the standoff.

⚖️ Work with the attorney

When probate, a quiet title action or a partition case is needed, we coordinate with the estate's attorney and the court process rather than around it.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Do all heirs have to agree to sell an inherited house?

To sell the whole house, generally yes: everyone on title, or the estate's personal representative with authority, has to sign. But each heir usually owns a share they can sell on their own, and a court can order a sale through partition if the heirs stay deadlocked. Pages of Purpose LLC buys whole houses and individual heirs' shares.

Can you buy just my share of an inherited house?

In many cases, yes. If you hold an undivided interest as an heir or tenant in common, you can usually sell that interest without the others' permission. We make offers on partial interests and then work with the remaining heirs.

What if probate was never opened?

Then the house may still be legally owned by the person who died. We figure out what it takes to get authority to sell, often a small-estate procedure or a late probate filing, and work with an attorney to get it done.

What if we can't find one of the heirs?

We run the genealogical and skip-trace research to locate them. If someone truly can't be found, courts in most states have procedures to protect that heir's share and still allow a sale.

Are you lawyers?

No. Pages of Purpose LLC is a real estate company, not a law firm, and nothing we say is legal advice. We work alongside the estate's attorney, or one licensed where the house is.

Which states do you buy in?

All 50. We are based in Maryland and have state-specific heir guides for Texas, Florida, California, North Carolina and Maryland, plus city guides for inherited and probate houses.
