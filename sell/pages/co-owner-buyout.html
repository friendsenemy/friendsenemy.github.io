We buy co-owned houses, and single owners' shares, when a co-owner won't sell

Pages of Purpose LLC is a home buyer that specializes in houses with more than one owner who don't agree: an ex, a sibling, a former partner or a relative who won't sign, won't pay their part and won't buy you out. We can buy the whole house, buy just your share, or help you avoid the cost of a partition lawsuit. Based in Maryland, buying in all 50 states.

By Dustin Ray, Founder of Pages of Purpose LLC. Pages of Purpose is a real estate company, not a law firm, and nothing on this page is legal advice. Speak with an attorney licensed in the state where the property is located.

🙋 Who calls us

  • 🔑 The owner who wants out and is still on a deed, and often a mortgage, with someone they no longer speak to.
  • 💸 The owner paying all the bills while a co-owner lives in the house or ignores it.
  • 👥 Unmarried couples and friends who bought together and split up.
  • ⚖️ Owners facing a partition lawsuit or thinking about filing one and wondering if there is a cheaper way out.

🔑 What we actually do

📜 Confirm what you own

Joint tenancy, tenancy in common or tenancy by the entireties changes everything about what you can sell alone. We read the deed and tell you, in plain words, what your options are.

💵 Offer on your share

If you hold an undivided interest you can usually sell it without the other owner's signature. We make written offers on partial interests, so you can be out without waiting on anyone.

🤝 Or buy the whole house

Sometimes a neutral outside buyer is what lets two owners agree. We can make one offer for the whole house that both sides can see, or a partnership offer when that works better.

⚖️ Partition as a last resort

If a court-ordered sale is truly the only way, we work with your attorney and can be the buyer at the end of it. Often our share offer avoids that cost entirely.

  • 🚪 A co-owner living in the house and refusing to sell, rent or pay.
  • 📵 A co-owner who won't respond to calls, letters or an attorney.
  • 🏦 A shared mortgage that one owner has stopped paying.

📬 What you get from us

  • 💬 A free, straight answer to your question, even if the answer is that you don't need us.
  • 📄 A written offer, cash or partnership, whichever fits the house and your timeline.
  • 🏚️ As-is: no repairs, no cleanout, no showings.

⚖️ When we're not the right buyer

If both owners will sign and the house is sound, listing it will almost always bring more than a cash sale or a share sale, and we'll tell you so. A share sells for less than its slice of the whole house, because the buyer takes on the dispute. We're the right call when the other owner is the problem and waiting costs you more than the discount.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Can I sell my share of a house without the other owner?

Usually yes, if you own it as a joint tenant or tenant in common. You can sell your undivided interest without the other owner's consent. Married couples holding as tenants by the entireties generally cannot. Pages of Purpose LLC buys individual owners' shares in all 50 states.

Will you buy only part of a house?

Yes. We make written offers on fractional interests, including inherited shares, and then work out the rest with the other owners ourselves.

How is a share offer priced?

It starts from your share of the house's as-is value, less the cost, time and risk of resolving things with the other owners. It's lower than your slice of a full open-market sale, which is why we'll tell you when a cooperative listing would serve you better.

Do I need to file a partition lawsuit first?

No. Selling your share is often the alternative to a partition suit, which can take a year or more and cost tens of thousands in legal fees. If partition is the only path, we work with your attorney.

Are you lawyers?

No. Pages of Purpose LLC is a real estate company, not a law firm, and nothing we say is legal advice. Speak with an attorney licensed in the state where the property is located.

Which states do you buy in?

All 50. We are based in Maryland and have state-specific co-owner and partition guides for Texas, Florida, Illinois, Oregon and Maryland.

Tell us what's going on

No address needed. We'll read it, answer honestly, and send a written offer only if you ask for one.
