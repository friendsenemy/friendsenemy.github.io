Selling a Crofton House That Came With a Problem Attached

Crofton is not its own city. It is an unincorporated community in western Anne Arundel County, built up as a planned suburb around the Route 3 and Route 424 corridor near Route 50, with Annapolis, Bowie and the Baltimore and Washington job markets all within a commute. For a seller that has two practical effects: there is no separate town government adding a municipal tax to the bill, and every court and land-record question runs through Anne Arundel County offices in Annapolis.

Crofton Is Governed From Annapolis, Not From a Town Hall

If the owner of a Crofton house has died, the estate is opened with the Anne Arundel County Register of Wills in Annapolis, and disputes go before the Orphans' Court there. Deeds, liens and judgments are recorded at the county level as well. When a family tells us "the house is in Crofton," the first question we ask is whether anyone has opened the estate yet, because nobody can give a buyer clean title until a personal representative has been appointed or the property has passed some other way, such as a deed with a survivorship clause.

Maryland lets smaller estates use a simplified process: if the property that goes through probate is worth $50,000 or less (or $100,000 when the surviving spouse is the only heir), the paperwork is lighter. Most Crofton houses are worth more than that, so most families here go through regular administration.

What a Vacant Crofton House Costs to Hold

Because Crofton has no municipal rate, the FY2027 bill (July 2026 through June 2027) is the Anne Arundel County rate of $0.968 plus the state rate of $0.112, a total of $1.08 per $100 of assessed value. On a house assessed at $415,000, that comes to $4,482 a year, or about $374 a month. That number keeps running whether anyone lives there or not, and it does not include insurance on a vacant dwelling, utilities to keep pipes from freezing, or any homeowners association dues if the house sits in an HOA community.

Anne Arundel County caps how fast an owner-occupant's taxable assessment can rise at 2% a year through the Homestead Tax Credit. That protection belongs to the person who lives in the house as a principal residence. An heir who inherits and keeps the property as a vacant or rental house generally does not qualify, so the taxable value can climb toward the full assessment over time.

Things that often stall a Crofton sale

  • A parent's house that has not been updated in decades: original kitchen, aging roof, old HVAC, a basement that took on water.
  • Siblings who agree on selling but not on price, or one sibling living in the house rent-free.
  • HOA violation letters or unpaid dues that turn into a lien.
  • A mortgage or reverse mortgage with a payoff clock running after the owner's death.

When You Should List With an Agent Instead

If the Crofton house is in good shape, the owners agree, and there is no deadline from a lender, a court or a tax bill, a local listing agent will very likely net you more than any cash buyer. Buyers in this part of the county pay retail for clean, move-in-ready homes. We tell people that plainly. Where we are useful is the house that would not pass a buyer's inspection without real work, or the sale that is held up by an estate, a co-owner or a lien.
