Selling a Temple Hills House When an Estate, Debt or Repairs Are Involved

Inherited Houses in Temple Hills Start in Upper Marlboro

Temple Hills is an unincorporated community in southern Prince George's County, just outside the District line. Many of its brick ramblers, split-levels and colonials were bought by families in the decades after World War II and stayed in the family. When the owner dies, the next step happens at the Prince George's County Register of Wills in Upper Marlboro, where the estate is opened and the Orphans' Court appoints a personal representative. Nobody else, not even the child who has been paying the bills, can sign a deed until that appointment is made.

Maryland's small-estate process covers estates with probate assets of $50,000 or less, or $100,000 or less when the surviving spouse is the sole heir. A Temple Hills house will often push an estate over those limits, which means regular administration. If there is no will, Maryland's intestacy rules decide the heirs, and every one of them has a stake. Our article on what happens to a house when someone dies without a will explains how that plays out. A Maryland estate attorney can answer questions specific to your family.

Tax Bills Without a Town Rate

Because Temple Hills is not an incorporated town, owners pay only Prince George's County's and the state's rates. For FY2027, the county rate here is $1.00 per $100 of assessed value and the state adds $0.112, for $1.112 in total. On a house assessed at $260,000, the yearly bill is about $2,891, or $241 a month.

The county's Homestead Tax Credit cap is 3%, limiting how much an owner-occupied home's taxable assessment can grow each year. An heir who inherits but lives elsewhere generally does not get that credit. If taxes go unpaid long enough, Prince George's County can sell a lien on the property at its tax sale, adding interest and legal fees on top.

Behind on the Mortgage or Facing Foreclosure

We also hear from Temple Hills owners who fell behind after a job change, a medical problem or a death in the family. Maryland foreclosures follow a set process with notices before a sale date, and selling before the auction can protect whatever equity is left. Our guide to facing foreclosure in Maryland lays out the timing. Pages of Purpose LLC, run by Dustin Ray, can review the payoff, the liens and the house's condition to see whether a sale leaves you money after the lender is paid.

  • Houses with an aging roof, HVAC or plumbing the family cannot afford to replace.
  • Co-owners or ex-spouses who need to divide the proceeds.
  • Rentals with tenants who have stopped paying.

The Case for Listing With an Agent

If the house is in good condition, every owner agrees to sell, and there is no foreclosure or tax deadline, a local agent will usually get you a higher price than a cash buyer. That is worth saying clearly. A direct sale makes more sense when the house needs work that financed buyers cannot take on, when time is short, or when an estate or title problem would scare off a typical buyer.
