Selling a Hollywood, Maryland Home Near Pax River and the Patuxent

Hollywood is an unincorporated community in northern St. Mary's County, along Route 235 between Mechanicsville and the Lexington Park area, with the Patuxent River on its eastern side. Historic Sotterley Plantation sits on the river here. Many people who live in Hollywood work at or around Naval Air Station Patuxent River, a short drive south, and that connection explains a lot of the house sales: orders to a new duty station, a contractor's job ending, or a retiree moving closer to family.

Leaving Pax River: Sell, Rent or Partner?

If you have orders and your Hollywood house is in good shape, a local listing agent will usually get you the most money. That is the straight answer. The calculation changes when the house needs a roof, a septic repair or a full update that you cannot manage from another state, or when the report date is fixed and a buyer's financing could fall through. Our guide on selling a Maryland house on PCS orders lays out what to compare.

Renting the house out is another choice, and plenty of Hollywood owners do it. Before you commit, price a property manager, plan for vacancies, and remember that the homestead credit described below generally ends once the house is no longer your home.

Rural Lots, Wells and Waterfront Rules

Outside the newer subdivisions, Hollywood is rural. Many homes sit on larger lots with private wells and septic systems, and some have outbuildings or additions with uncertain permit histories. Properties along the Patuxent and its creeks may fall within Maryland's Critical Area, the land within 1,000 feet of tidal waters, where the county limits clearing, lot coverage and building near the shoreline. A buyer planning to expand or rebuild a waterfront house needs to know those limits up front.

Tax Math for a Hollywood House in FY2027

Hollywood has no town government, so only county and state rates apply: $0.8478 for St. Mary's County and $0.112 for the state, totaling $0.9598 per $100 of assessed value. A house assessed at $310,000 owes about $2,975 a year, or $248 a month.

St. Mary's County caps yearly increases in an owner-occupant's taxable assessment at 3% through the Homestead Tax Credit. An heir or landlord who does not live there generally does not receive it.

When a Hollywood Owner Passes Away

The estate is opened with the St. Mary's County Register of Wills in Leonardtown, the county seat, and disagreements go before the Orphans' Court there. Until a personal representative is appointed, no one can sign a deed for the estate. Maryland's small-estate process is available when the probate property is $50,000 or less, or $100,000 if the surviving spouse is the only heir.

Common estate snags we see in this area

  • Family land divided informally among relatives without new deeds.
  • A house where one heir has lived for years and others want their share.
  • Older manufactured homes on family parcels, with title questions about the home itself.
