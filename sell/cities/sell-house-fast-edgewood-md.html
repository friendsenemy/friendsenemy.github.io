Edgewood, Harford County: Selling an Older House That Needs Work

Older housing between two rivers

Edgewood lies in southern Harford County between the Bush River and the Gunpowder River, next to the Edgewood Area of Aberdeen Proving Ground. Much of its housing went up decades ago to serve the installation and the commuters along the Route 40 and I-95 corridor: townhouses, small ranchers and split-levels that have now been through several owners or one very long one.

Age is the common thread in the Edgewood calls we get. Original electrical panels, aging furnaces, flat or worn roofs, and basements that seep after heavy rain are typical. None of that makes a house unsellable, but it does narrow who can buy it. Many buyers using FHA or VA loans need the house to meet minimum property standards, and an appraiser can flag peeling paint, missing handrails or a failing roof as conditions that must be fixed before closing.

Repairs that most often stop a financed sale

  • A roof at the end of its life or with active leaks
  • Heating that doesn't work or is unsafe
  • Exposed or outdated wiring
  • Water in the basement, mold, or a damaged foundation wall

We buy with those problems in place. You don't have to fix them first.

Harford County tax on an Edgewood house

Edgewood has no town government, so there is no municipal tax. The FY2027 rates are Harford County at $0.9779 and the State of Maryland at $0.112 per $100 of assessed value, together $1.0899.

For an Edgewood house assessed at $215,000, the yearly bill is $2,343, about $195 a month. On a vacant house waiting for repairs that never seem to start, that is one of several monthly costs that keep accumulating.

Harford's Homestead Tax Credit limits the rise in an owner-occupant's taxable assessment to 5% a year. That benefit is tied to the person living in the house as a principal residence; heirs and landlords who don't live there generally don't receive it.

Estates go to Bel Air

Harford County's Register of Wills and Orphans' Court are in Bel Air, the county seat. If the owner has died, a personal representative appointed there usually signs the deed. If the house has fallen behind on taxes during that time, the county can include it in its tax sale, and the interest and fees grow from there. Our guide on selling a Maryland house with liens or back taxes explains what gets paid at settlement.

When you'll do better with a listing

If your Edgewood house has been updated and passes inspection, the sellers agree, and you have time, an agent will likely get you more. A cash or partnership offer from us is worth comparing when the repairs are extensive, the estate or title is unresolved, there are liens, or nobody can manage the property any longer.

Two ways to sell

With a cash offer we buy as-is and, if the title is clear and the sale is simple, can close in as few as 7 days. With a partnership offer we pay for the repairs and the sellers share in the price when the renovated house sells. Dustin Ray reviews the land records and liens before either offer is made. See our page on liens and title problems if your issue is paperwork rather than repairs.
