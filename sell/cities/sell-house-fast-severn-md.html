Selling a Severn Home When Orders, Tenants or Repairs Set the Clock

Severn borders Fort George G. Meade and sits a short drive from BWI Marshall Airport, which shapes who owns houses here and why they sell. A lot of Severn owners are service members, defense contractors and federal workers. When permanent change of station orders arrive, the owner often has weeks, not months, to decide whether to sell, rent the house out from another state, or hand it to a property manager.

PCS Orders and the Rent-or-Sell Decision

Renting out a Severn house after a move can make sense if the mortgage is low and the house is in good condition. It becomes a problem when the owner is managing from far away, the tenant stops paying, or a repair comes up that nobody local can supervise. We hear from a fair number of Severn owners who rented the house when they left and now want out from a few time zones away.

If you are on orders right now and the house is in good shape, list it. An agent can usually get more for a clean house near Fort Meade than a cash buyer will pay. A cash or partnership offer is worth comparing when the house needs work you cannot oversee, when you want a set closing date before you report, or when there is a tenant in place who may not cooperate with showings. Our guide on selling a Maryland house on PCS orders goes through the timeline and the questions to ask any buyer.

The Carrying Cost of a Severn House in FY2027

Severn is unincorporated Anne Arundel County, so there is no town tax. The FY2027 rates are $0.968 for the county and $0.112 for the state, or $1.08 per $100 of assessed value. A house assessed at $340,000 owes $3,672 a year, about $306 a month, on top of mortgage, insurance and upkeep. For an owner who has already moved, that is money spent on a house they no longer use.

The county's Homestead Tax Credit limits yearly increases in an owner-occupant's taxable assessment to 2%. Once you move out and rent the house, it is no longer your principal residence, and the credit generally ends. The same is true for an heir who inherits a Severn house and does not live in it.

Tired of being a long-distance landlord?

Selling a rented house is possible with the tenant still there. Maryland leases generally survive a sale, so a buyer usually takes over the lease rather than forcing a move-out. We buy occupied houses and deal with the tenant directly after closing. Our article for tired landlords selling rental property in Maryland covers notice, deposits and what to hand over at settlement.

Estates and Title Questions in Severn

When a Severn owner dies, the estate is handled by the Anne Arundel County Register of Wills in Annapolis. Maryland's simplified small-estate process applies when the probate property is worth $50,000 or less, or $100,000 if the surviving spouse is the only heir; a house usually pushes an estate past that. Until a personal representative is appointed, nobody has authority to sign a deed. We can make an offer while the estate is being opened and set the closing for after the appointment.
