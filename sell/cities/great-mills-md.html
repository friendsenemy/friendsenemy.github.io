Selling a Great Mills Home Near NAS Patuxent River

The lowest-rate county in Southern Maryland, and what it still costs

Great Mills is an unincorporated community in St. Mary's County, next to Lexington Park and a short drive from NAS Patuxent River. Of the Southern Maryland counties, St. Mary's has a comparatively low property tax rate, and that is worth knowing before you decide to hold onto a house you aren't using.

For FY2027, St. Mary's County levies $0.8478 per $100 of assessed value and the state adds $0.112, for a combined $0.9598. There is no town tax because Great Mills is not incorporated.

A house assessed at $265,000 owes $2,543 a year at that rate, roughly $212 a month. That is lower than in many neighboring counties, but it never stops. Combined with insurance on a vacant home and basic upkeep, an empty house in Great Mills can still cost an estate several thousand dollars a year.

The 3% homestead cap

St. Mary's County caps annual increases in an owner-occupant's taxable assessment at 3% under the Homestead Tax Credit. A long-time owner may be paying tax on a value well below the current assessment. When that owner dies or moves out, and an heir or landlord who doesn't live there takes over, the credit generally ends. The jump can be noticeable, which is one reason families revisit whether to keep a parent's house.

Base-driven moves and rentals

Pax River brings a steady flow of service members, civil servants and contractors in and out of the area. Many Great Mills homes have been rentals at some point. Common reasons owners call us:

  • A landlord tired of turnover and repairs between tenants
  • A transfer that leaves a house half-finished or unrented
  • A rental with a tenant who has stopped paying
  • A family home where the parents have died and the children live out of state

If you own a rental here and want out, our page for tired landlords explains how we handle a sale with a tenant in place.

Probate in Leonardtown

St. Mary's County estates are filed with the Register of Wills and the Orphans' Court in Leonardtown, the county seat. Before a house titled to someone who has died can be sold, the court normally appoints a personal representative. Maryland's small-estate process applies when probate assets are $50,000 or less, or $100,000 or less when the surviving spouse is the only heir. Our St. Mary's County page has more on selling across the county, and this article on why some St. Mary's owners take cash offers lays out the tradeoffs.

Listing versus selling to us

Plenty of Great Mills houses should simply be listed. With steady demand from base employees, a house in good condition, with owners who agree and no deadline, usually brings more through an agent. We fit when the house needs heavy repairs, a tenant situation needs to be resolved, the title has gaps, or the estate is stuck.

Our offer can be cash, or a partnership where we fund the work and you share in the finished sale. A simple sale with clear title can close in as few as 7 days. Dustin Ray reviews the land records and any liens himself before any number is put in writing.
