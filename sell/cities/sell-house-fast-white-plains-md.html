Selling Your White Plains Home: Estates in La Plata and What It Costs to Wait

Five minutes from the courthouse that handles your estate

White Plains is an unincorporated community in Charles County, sitting along the US 301 corridor between Waldorf and La Plata. La Plata is the county seat, and that is where the Charles County Register of Wills and Orphans' Court are located. If you have inherited a White Plains house, the office that will open the estate, appoint the personal representative and approve the paperwork is close by, which helps when you need to file in person or pick up letters of administration.

Maryland's small-estate process applies only when probate assets are $50,000 or less ($100,000 when a surviving spouse is the only heir). With a house in the estate, most families need regular administration, and the personal representative is the one who signs a sales contract and deed.

Common estate snags

  • The house was in a parent's name only and the surviving parent has since died too.
  • No will, so every heir under Maryland law has a say.
  • A relative is living in the house rent-free.
  • A reverse mortgage that becomes due after the borrower's death.

What an empty White Plains house costs each month

There is no town government here, so owners pay only the Charles County rate plus the state rate. For FY2027: county $1.141 + state $0.112 = $1.253 per $100 of assessed value.

A home assessed at $365,000 carries a tax bill of about $4,573 a year, around $381 a month. While an estate is being settled, that comes out of the estate or the heirs' pockets, along with insurance, SMECO power and upkeep.

Charles County's Homestead Tax Credit caps a resident owner's yearly assessment growth at 7%. An heir who keeps the home without living in it generally does not get the credit.

Commuter homes, PCS orders and life changes

Many White Plains households commute toward Washington, D.C., Joint Base Andrews or Naval Support Facility Indian Head. A job change or military transfer can force a sale on a schedule that does not line up with the market. If you have orders in hand, our article on selling on PCS orders in Maryland covers the timing choices. Divorce brings similar pressure; a Maryland family law attorney can explain how the house is handled before you agree to anything.

A listing may serve you better

If your White Plains home is in good condition, the owners agree on a price, and there is no hard deadline, hire a local agent and list it. In that situation the open market usually beats a cash offer, and we would say so if you called.

Selling directly tends to make sense when the house needs a new roof, HVAC or septic work a lender will not accept, when the owners are in conflict, or when liens and back taxes need to be paid off from the sale.
