Selling a House in Upper Marlboro, Where Prince George's County Estates Are Filed

The courthouse town

Upper Marlboro is the county seat of Prince George's County. The county courthouse is here, and so are the Register of Wills and the Orphans' Court. For anyone selling a house that belonged to someone who died anywhere in the county, from Bowie to Fort Washington, this is where the estate is opened and where a personal representative gets the authority to sell.

That makes Upper Marlboro a useful place to start even if the house isn't here. If it is here, you're a short drive from the office that controls your timeline.

How the estate side usually goes

  1. Someone, usually a family member named in the will, files to open the estate.
  2. The Register of Wills appoints a personal representative and issues letters of administration.
  3. The PR handles the house: securing it, insuring it, paying taxes, and, when ready, signing a sale contract and deed.

Estates below $50,000, or $100,000 when a surviving spouse is the sole heir, can often use Maryland's small-estate process. Without a will, state law decides who inherits. Our page on selling an inherited house in Maryland covers more. Ask a Maryland estate attorney about your particular estate.

The town itself is small, and it taxes separately

The incorporated town of Upper Marlboro is small, and most addresses with an Upper Marlboro mailing label are actually outside town limits in unincorporated Prince George's. That distinction matters for taxes. Inside the town, owners pay a town rate on top of county and state.

For a house in town assessed at $310,000, the FY2027 rates (county $0.930 + town $0.380 + state $0.112 = $1.422 per $100) come to about $4,408 a year, roughly $367 a month. Outside town limits, the town portion drops off.

The homestead cap listed for Upper Marlboro is 0%, which means an owner-occupant's taxable assessment does not rise year to year while the credit applies. For a long-time owner, the gap between the taxed value and the real value can be large. When the house passes to an heir who doesn't live there, that credit generally ends and the bill can jump to reflect the full assessment.

Houses with acreage and older systems

Around the town, much of the land is rural or semi-rural, with houses on larger lots, some on wells and septic, and some older farmhouses. Equestrian properties and homes along the Patuxent side of the county show up too. Rural systems, outbuildings and long driveways add items a buyer's inspector will flag. If you're dealing with land or a lot more than a house, see our page on selling vacant land.

Listing versus a cash or partnership offer

A house in good repair with willing sellers and no deadline usually does best on the open market with a local agent. That's true here as much as anywhere.

Pages of Purpose is a better match when an estate has stalled, heirs disagree, the house needs major work, or there's a lien or tax problem the family can't resolve alone. Our offer can be cash, or a partnership when that works out better for the owners. A simple cash sale can close in as few as 7 days.
