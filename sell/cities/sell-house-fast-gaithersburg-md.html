Selling a Gaithersburg House: An Honest Look at Your Options

Should a Gaithersburg Owner Sell to a Cash Buyer?

Start with the honest answer: most Gaithersburg homeowners should not. Gaithersburg is a large incorporated city in Montgomery County, home to the National Institute of Standards and Technology and a steady base of buyers working along the I-270 corridor. If your house is in reasonable shape, everyone on the deed agrees, and you are not up against a deadline, listing with a local agent will very likely put more money in your pocket than any cash offer, including ours.

Where a direct sale makes sense is the house that does not fit the retail picture: an estate that cannot be settled until the house is sold, owners who disagree, a property with years of deferred maintenance, liens or back taxes, or an owner who is in jail or otherwise unable to manage a listing. Pages of Purpose LLC, run by Dustin Ray, works on those cases with either a cash offer or a partnership offer.

Three Tax Rates on One Bill

Because Gaithersburg is incorporated, owners inside city limits pay a city property tax in addition to the county and state rates. For FY2027:

Taxing bodyRate per $100 assessed
Montgomery County$0.6706
City of Gaithersburg$0.262
State of Maryland$0.112
Combined$1.0446

For a house assessed at $540,000, that comes to about $5,641 a year, or $470 a month. Some addresses with a Gaithersburg mailing address are actually outside city limits and do not pay the city portion; the tax bill will show which applies.

The Homestead Tax Credit cap here is 10%. It sets a ceiling on how much an owner-occupant's taxable assessment can rise each year. An heir who does not live in the house generally loses the credit, so expect the bill to reflect the full assessment over time.

Estates and the Register of Wills in Rockville

Gaithersburg residents' estates are handled by the Montgomery County Register of Wills and Orphans' Court in Rockville. A personal representative must be appointed before the house can be sold. Maryland's small-estate route applies only up to $50,000 in probate assets, or $100,000 if the surviving spouse is the sole heir, which rules out almost any Gaithersburg house. A regular estate means notice to creditors, an inventory and court filings. A Maryland estate attorney can guide the family through them.

When the heirs do not agree

A common Gaithersburg situation is a parent's house shared by several adult children, some wanting to sell and some wanting to keep it or rent it. That can freeze a sale for a long time. Our page on houses where heirs disagree explains what a buyer can and cannot do, and when a partition case becomes the only option.

Houses That Need More Than Paint

Gaithersburg has a wide range of homes, from older single-family houses near the original town center to townhouse communities and planned neighborhoods. The ones that land on our desk usually have a problem a retail buyer's lender will not accept: a failing roof, foundation movement, water damage, hoarding, or unpermitted work. You can get a written cash offer, or a partnership offer if that leaves you with more.

  • We check the land records, liens and the tax account before we give a number.
  • We explain the difference between the cash figure and the partnership figure in writing.
  • Simple sales can close in as few as 7 days; estate sales follow the court's schedule.
