Greenbelt Homes and the City Tax Most Buyers Ask About

Greenbelt is one of the few places in Prince George's County where the property tax bill has three layers instead of two. It is an incorporated city with its own government, so homeowners pay a Greenbelt city tax on top of the county and state rates. For FY2027 that city rate is $0.8275 per $100 of assessed value, close to the county's own rate, and it changes the math for anyone holding an empty house while an estate is settled or repairs are planned.

Three Tax Rates on One Greenbelt Bill

Prince George's County$0.875$2,275

Total$1.8145about $4,718 a year ($393 a month)

The same house in an unincorporated part of the county would owe about $2,566 a year at $0.987 per $100, so the city portion adds roughly $2,152. That is the price of city services, and buyers factor it in. For a family carrying a vacant house, it is a monthly cost to keep in view.

Prince George's County's Homestead Tax Credit limits yearly increases in an owner-occupant's taxable assessment to 10%. An heir who does not live in the house generally does not get that cap, and the credit ends when the house stops being the owner's principal residence.

A Planned Town With Its Own Kind of Housing

Greenbelt was built in the 1930s as a federal New Deal planned community, and the original section still shows it: rows of attached homes around shared green space and walking paths. Part of that original housing belongs to Greenbelt Homes, Inc., a housing cooperative. In a co-op, the occupant holds a membership in the cooperative rather than a separate deed to the unit, and the co-op has its own rules for transfers, inspections and approval of buyers. If you inherited a co-op home, start by asking the cooperative what its transfer process requires, because a standard house sale does not work the same way.

Elsewhere, Greenbelt has single-family houses, townhouse communities and condominiums, plus apartment complexes. NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center, the Greenbelt Metro station and the Capital Beltway are all nearby, so the city draws federal workers and commuters.

Estates and Upper Marlboro

When a Greenbelt owner dies, the estate is filed with the Prince George's County Register of Wills in Upper Marlboro, and the Orphans' Court there handles disputes. The personal representative it appoints is the one who signs. Maryland's simplified procedure applies to estates with probate property of $50,000 or less, or $100,000 when a surviving spouse is the only heir. Should You Sell to a Cash Buyer?

Often, no. If your Greenbelt home is in good condition, the owners agree and nothing is forcing a quick sale, a local listing agent will usually net you more. Expect a written cash figure, and a partnership option when it would put more in your pocket. Our article on how cash home buyers work in Prince George's County shows how offers are figured, and our Prince George's County page covers the wider county.
