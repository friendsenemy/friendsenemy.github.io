Jacksonville Houses With Problems: Duval County Estates, Taxes and Auctions

Jacksonville and Duval County share one consolidated government, which means one Clerk of Courts, one Property Appraiser and one tax roll for almost the whole county. For a seller with a problem house, that simplifies the paperwork but speeds up the clock: when a Duval mortgage case reaches judgment, the house is sold online on the Clerk's auction site, and those auctions run every weekday starting at 11:00 a.m.

How a Duval Foreclosure Ends: The Online Auction

Florida foreclosure is judicial. The lender has to sue in circuit court and win a final judgment before anything is sold. Under section 45.031 of the Florida Statutes, that judgment sets the sale for a date not less than 20 days and not more than 35 days after it is entered, unless the lender agrees to a later date. In Duval the bidding happens at duval.realforeclose.com, not on the courthouse steps.

After the sale, any party has 10 days to object to the bid. If no one objects, the Clerk issues a certificate of title and ownership passes to the buyer. Money left over after the debt is paid is called surplus, and the owner of record has to file a claim with the court to get it. Selling before the auction date usually keeps more of your equity than waiting for a surplus check, but it only works if a closing can happen inside that 20-to-35-day window or earlier.

  • Read the final judgment for the exact sale date.
  • Ask the lender's attorney for a written payoff.
  • Line up a buyer who can close with cash, without a loan contingency.

Inherited Jacksonville Houses and the Fourth Circuit

Estates in Duval County are opened in the Probate Division of the Fourth Judicial Circuit Court, with filings through the Duval County Clerk. Florida offers summary administration, a shorter court process, when the estate subject to administration is worth $75,000 or less (not counting exempt property) or when the person has been dead more than two years. Plenty of Jacksonville families qualify under the two-year rule because a parent's house sat untouched for years before anyone tried to sell.

A house that was the owner's homestead adds a layer. Florida's constitution limits who can receive a homestead when the owner leaves a spouse or minor child, so the will may not control. Our guide on Florida homestead rules when heirs can't agree walks through those limits, and selling only your share of an inherited Florida house covers the option of exiting without the other heirs.

Reading a Duval Tax Bill

Florida taxes in mills, meaning dollars per $1,000 of taxable value. For 2025 the Property Appraiser's final chart shows 17.7412 mills for USD 1, the general Jacksonville district:

LevyMills
City/County general government11.1919
Duval County Public Schools6.3430
St. Johns River Water Management District0.1793
Florida Inland Navigation District0.0270
Total17.7412

Worked example: an inherited Jacksonville house with no homestead exemption and a taxable value of $200,000 would owe 200,000 ÷ 1,000 × 17.7412 = $3,548.24 for the year. Jacksonville Beach, Neptune Beach, Atlantic Beach and Baldwin have their own city millage, so check which district your parcel sits in. When a homesteaded parent dies, the exemption and its assessment cap do not pass automatically to heirs, so the bill on an inherited house can rise after the next January 1 reassessment.
