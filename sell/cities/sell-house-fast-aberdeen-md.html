Selling an Aberdeen House During a PCS Move, Estate or Separation

Aberdeen Proving Ground and Sudden Moves

Aberdeen is an incorporated city in Harford County, next to Aberdeen Proving Ground, one of the Army's major research and testing installations. That shapes the local housing market. Service members and civilian employees receive permanent change of station orders, and some owners find themselves trying to sell or manage a house from another state on short notice. Others decide to rent the house out, then later want to sell after a few rounds of tenant turnover and wear.

Options when orders come

  • List it with an agent before you leave, if there is time and the house is ready.
  • Rent it, knowing you will be a long-distance landlord.
  • Sell directly if repairs, a tenant still in place, or the timeline make a listing hard.

Aberdeen's City Tax Adds to the Monthly Cost

Owners inside Aberdeen city limits pay a city real property tax along with Harford County's. The FY2027 rates (July 2026 through June 2027) are $0.8413 per $100 for the county, $0.54 for the city and $0.112 for the state, for a total of $1.4933 per $100.

For a house assessed at $265,000, that is about $3,957 a year, or around $330 a month. An owner who has moved away and is paying a new mortgage or rent elsewhere feels that bill quickly.

The Homestead Tax Credit in Aberdeen caps yearly increases in an owner-occupant's taxable assessment at 10%. It requires that the home be the owner's principal residence. Someone who relocates on orders and rents the house out, or an heir who inherits it while living elsewhere, should check whether they still qualify, because generally they do not.

Harford County Estates and Inherited Houses

When an Aberdeen homeowner passes away, the estate is opened with the Harford County Register of Wills in Bel Air, the county seat, and the Orphans' Court supervises it. The personal representative signs for the estate. The small-estate route applies if probate assets are $50,000 or less, or up to $100,000 when the surviving spouse is the only heir. For most estates that include a house, regular administration is the path, and the will decides whether the representative can sell without each heir's consent. See how to sell an inherited house in Maryland for the step-by-step.

Divorce, Liens and Foreclosure

Other Aberdeen situations we hear about include spouses who separate while both are on the deed, houses with a judgment or contractor's lien, and owners who have fallen behind on the mortgage. If a foreclosure has started, timing matters; our article on selling a Maryland house before the foreclosure auction explains the steps. For divorce or estate questions, a Maryland attorney is the right source; this page is not legal advice.

When a Listing Will Net More

To be direct about it: a house in good condition, owners who agree, and a seller with a few months to spare will usually do better on the open market with a Harford County agent. Military buyers coming into APG use VA and other financing that rewards a house in good repair.
