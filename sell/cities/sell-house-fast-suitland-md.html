Inherited a Suitland House? Probate in Upper Marlboro and Your Options

Suitland is an unincorporated community in Prince George's County, home to the Suitland Federal Center where the U.S. Census Bureau has its headquarters, and close to Joint Base Andrews and the District line. Because it is not an incorporated town, there is no separate municipal tax, which keeps carrying costs lower than in many nearby places. The more common hurdle for Suitland sellers who reach out to us is an estate: a parent or grandparent has died, the house is still in their name, and the family is not sure who can sign.

Probate starts at the courthouse in Upper Marlboro

Prince George's County estates are opened with the Register of Wills in Upper Marlboro, the county seat, where the Orphans' Court also sits. Until an estate is opened and a personal representative is appointed, nobody has the legal authority to sell a house that is titled only in the name of the person who died.

  1. Find out how the deed is titled. If the house was owned jointly with a spouse as tenants by the entirety, it may pass to the survivor without probate.
  2. Check the estate's size. Maryland allows a small estate filing when probate assets are $50,000 or less, or $100,000 or less when a surviving spouse is the only heir.
  3. Get a personal representative appointed. That person signs the sale contract and the deed.
  4. Clear what is owed. Mortgage, tax arrears, water bills and any liens are paid from the proceeds at closing.

When there was no will at all, Maryland's intestacy rules decide who inherits. Our explainer on what happens to a house when someone dies without a will goes into that. For legal questions specific to the estate, talk with a Maryland estates attorney.

Suitland property tax on a house nobody lives in

Since Suitland has no town government, only two rates apply in FY2027: Prince George's County at $1.000 and the State of Maryland at $0.112, a total of $1.112 per $100 of assessed value.

For a house assessed at $235,000, the yearly bill would be about $2,613, or $218 a month. That is modest compared with Baltimore City, but it does not stop while the estate is open, and it sits alongside insurance, the water and sewer bill, and the lawn.

The county's Homestead Tax Credit cap of 3% limits how much the taxable assessment on an owner-occupied home can rise each year. When the house passes to an heir who does not live there, that credit usually ends, so the taxable value can start to catch up with the full assessment. If taxes fall behind long enough, the county can put the lien in its tax sale.

When family members do not agree

With inherited houses near the D.C. line, it is common to have several heirs in different states, one sibling living in the house, and no agreement on what it is worth. Pages of Purpose can make a cash offer for the whole property, or in some cases buy one heir's share. Our guide to whether all heirs have to agree to sell in Maryland explains the partition process if it comes to that, and the Prince George's County page covers the county as a whole.

Listing versus a cash or partnership offer

If the Suitland house is updated, everyone agrees and there is no time pressure, list it with a local agent. You will likely net more. A cash offer is worth considering when the house needs major work, someone is still living there and needs time to move, there are liens or back taxes, or the heirs want one clean transaction. A partnership offer can fund the repairs and split a retail sale. A simple sale can close in as few as 7 days; an estate sale moves at the pace of the court.
