Selling a Leonardtown Home in St. Mary's County's Seat on Breton Bay

The Register of Wills is right here in town

Leonardtown is the county seat of St. Mary's County, so the Register of Wills and the Orphans' Court for the whole county are in town. For a family settling an estate, that is convenient: filings, hearings and questions about a personal representative's authority all happen a short drive away rather than in another county.

Maryland's small-estate process covers probate assets of $50,000 or less, or $100,000 when a surviving spouse is the sole heir. A house in or around Leonardtown usually exceeds that, which means a regular estate with a personal representative who can sign the deed. If you are new to it, our guide on selling an inherited house in Maryland covers the steps. For questions specific to your estate or a divorce, talk to a Maryland attorney.

A town on tidal water

Leonardtown sits at the head of Breton Bay, a tidal arm of the Potomac, with a public wharf at the water's edge. Property within 1,000 feet of tidal water falls under Maryland's Critical Area law, which limits clearing, paving and new construction close to the shore. On an older house near the water, that can affect what a buyer is able to add or rebuild and how much they will pay.

Outside the town limits, much of the surrounding county is rural, with homes on wells and septic systems that need to pass inspection before most lenders will finance a purchase.

Town, county and state: the FY2027 math

Leonardtown is an incorporated town with its own small property tax. For FY2027 (July 2026 to June 2027), owners pay St. Mary's County's $0.8478, the Town of Leonardtown's $0.1203, and the state's $0.112, for $1.0801 per $100 of assessed value. On a house assessed at $310,000, that is about $3,348 a year, roughly $279 a month, whether the house is occupied or not.

The local Homestead Tax Credit cap is 10%, meaning an owner-occupant's taxable assessment cannot rise more than 10% in a year. An heir who inherits and does not live there generally does not receive the credit.

Who sells in Leonardtown, and why

Naval Air Station Patuxent River in Lexington Park, elsewhere in the county, brings in military and contractor families who move on orders. Some of them bought in or near Leonardtown and later need to sell quickly or manage a rental from far away. See selling on PCS orders for that situation. Other sellers are long-time county families handling a parent's house, sometimes with siblings who disagree about what to do.

Before you call anyone

  • Pull the property's SDAT record to confirm the owner of record and assessment.
  • Find out whether there is a mortgage, reverse mortgage or lien.
  • Talk with the other heirs or co-owners about what each of them wants.

Sometimes a local agent is the better answer

If the house is in good condition, everyone on the deed agrees, and there is no deadline, listing with a Leonardtown-area agent will usually bring the highest price.
