Bryans Road House Sales: Military Moves, Estates and Charles County Taxes

On the road to Indian Head

Bryans Road is an unincorporated community in western Charles County, along Route 210 on the way to the Town of Indian Head and Naval Support Facility Indian Head. The Potomac River and Mattawoman Creek are close by. That location shapes who sells here: Navy civilians and service members who get orders elsewhere, longtime families whose parents bought decades ago, and commuters who drove 210 north toward the Beltway for years and are ready to stop.

Selling when orders arrive

A permanent change of station rarely waits for a house to be ready. If you are leaving Indian Head and your house needs work, or you have tenants in place, or you are already paying a mortgage in the next state, you have three realistic choices: list it, rent it from a distance, or sell it directly. Renting a house you can't see brings repair calls and vacancies at bad times. Listing works when the home is ready and you can leave it on the market while you are gone. A direct sale fits when the move date is fixed and the house isn't ready.

Charles County taxes, worked out

Because Bryans Road has no town government, there is no municipal tax. FY2027 rates are $1.141 per $100 for Charles County and $0.112 for the state, totaling $1.253 per $100 of assessed value.

For a house assessed at $295,000: 2,950 times $1.253 equals $3,696 a year, about $308 a month. An empty house in an estate, or a house left behind after a transfer, keeps generating that bill.

Charles County's Homestead Tax Credit caps annual growth in an owner-occupant's taxable assessment at 7%. When the owner moves away for good, or dies and the house goes to heirs who live elsewhere, the credit generally ends, and the bill can rise toward the full assessed value.

When a parent's house is part of an estate

Charles County estates go through the Register of Wills and Orphans' Court in La Plata. A personal representative appointed there signs the deed on the estate's behalf. Problems we see often in older Charles County homes:

  • The deed still lists a grandparent who died years ago, and that estate was never opened.
  • One sibling lives in the house and doesn't want to leave; the others need their share.
  • Property taxes fell behind during a long illness.
  • The house has original systems and needs more work than the family can fund.

Our page on selling when heirs disagree covers the disagreement problem. For an overview of the whole county, see our Charles County selling guide, and for signs it may be time to let go of a home here, read five signs it's time to sell a Charles County home.

Where an agent beats us

If your Bryans Road house is updated, the owners agree and you can wait for the right buyer, list it with a local agent. You will usually net more. We can make a cash offer, or a partnership offer where we pay for the work and you share in the sale. A clean sale with clear title can close in as few as 7 days.
