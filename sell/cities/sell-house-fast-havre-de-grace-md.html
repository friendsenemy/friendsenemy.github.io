Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to sell my house?

Our process is designed to be fast. Once you accept our offer, we can typically close within 7-30 days, depending on your needs and the title company's schedule.

Do I need to make repairs to sell my house?

No! We buy houses in any condition. Whether your home needs minor repairs or major renovations, we'll handle everything. This saves you time and money.

What is your offer based on?

Our offers are based on the current market value of your property, its condition, location, and comparable sales in the Havre de Grace area. We ensure our offers are fair and competitive.

Are there any hidden fees or commissions?

No. We believe in transparent pricing. There are no realtor commissions, no inspection fees, no appraisal fees, and no closing costs that you pay. What we offer is what you get.

Can you buy my house if I'm behind on payments?

Yes! We can help you avoid foreclosure by purchasing your property and handling any outstanding mortgage payments. Contact us immediately if you're facing financial hardship.
