Selling a Havre de Grace House Near the Susquehanna and the Bay

Where the Susquehanna Meets the Chesapeake

Havre de Grace sits where the Susquehanna River flows into the top of the Chesapeake Bay. That location is the reason many people love the city, and it also shapes what an owner can do with a property. Land within 1,000 feet of tidal water falls under Maryland's Critical Area law, which can restrict how much of a lot is covered by buildings and pavement, how close new construction can come to the shoreline, and whether a damaged structure near the water can be rebuilt larger or in a new spot. If you are selling a house near the waterfront, a buyer who plans to expand or rebuild will ask about it.

Pages of Purpose LLC, run by Dustin Ray, looks at waterfront and near-water houses carefully, including ones with flood damage, failing bulkheads or old additions. We can make a cash offer, or a partnership offer when that would net you more.

City, County and State Tax in Havre de Grace

Havre de Grace is an incorporated city in Harford County, so owners inside city limits pay three rates. For FY2027 those are $0.8413 to Harford County, $0.565 to the city and $0.112 to the state, a total of $1.5183 per $100 of assessed value. A house assessed at $285,000 pays about $4,327 a year, close to $361 a month. On a vacant or inherited house, that bill runs alongside flood insurance, if the property needs it, and routine upkeep.

The Homestead Tax Credit cap that applies here is 5%: an owner-occupant's taxable assessment cannot rise by more than that in a year. An heir or landlord who does not live in the house generally does not qualify, so the tax can jump after a sale or inheritance moves the house out of owner-occupied status.

Old Houses in a Historic Town

Downtown Havre de Grace has many older homes, some well over a hundred years old. They can have stone foundations, knob-and-tube wiring, lead paint, and porches and windows that need specialized repair. Houses in a historic district may also face review before exterior changes. Those issues can stretch out a retail sale, especially when the buyer's lender orders repairs.

Typical reasons owners call us

  • A rental that needs more work than the rent justifies.
  • Water intrusion, settling or other structural problems.
  • Liens, back taxes, or a mortgage that is behind.

Probate Is in Bel Air, Not Havre de Grace

Even though Havre de Grace is one of Harford County's larger cities, estates are handled at the Harford County Register of Wills in Bel Air, the county seat. The Orphans' Court there appoints the personal representative who signs the deed. Maryland's small-estate process is limited to probate assets of $50,000 or less, or $100,000 or less when the surviving spouse is the sole heir. For questions about a specific estate, talk with a Maryland attorney. Our Maryland inherited house guide covers the general steps.

When to List Instead

If your house is in sound condition, the owners agree, and there is no deadline, list it with a local agent. Havre de Grace's waterfront and historic charm draw retail buyers, and a well-maintained house will usually bring more on the open market than from us. We are a better fit for houses with repair, title, estate or timing problems.
