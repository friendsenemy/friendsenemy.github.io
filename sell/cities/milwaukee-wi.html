Selling a Milwaukee House: Heir Affidavits, Redemption Periods and Tax Bills

Wisconsin is one of the few states where a modest house can pass to an heir without a probate case at all. If everything the person owned in their own name is worth $50,000 or less, an heir can use a Transfer by Affidavit, and that affidavit can cover real estate. In parts of Milwaukee where older homes still sell for less than that, this one rule can save a family months.

Transfer by Affidavit for Small Milwaukee Estates

The affidavit is filed with the Register of Deeds in the county where the house sits, which for Milwaukee is the Milwaukee County Register of Deeds. Before recording, the heir has to give the other heirs at least 30 days' notice by certified mail or personal service, unless they all waive it. If the person who died received certain state-funded long-term care, the affidavit also goes to the state's Estate Recovery Program first. Only an heir, a trustee of the person's revocable trust or a former guardian can use it for real estate; a personal representative named in a will cannot.

When the estate is larger, the case goes to Milwaukee County Circuit Court, where the Register in Probate handles informal and formal administration. For estate or divorce questions, talk with a local attorney licensed in Wisconsin.

Marital Property Changes the Signature List

Wisconsin's marital property law works much like community property in Western states. Property acquired during a marriage is generally owned equally by both spouses, regardless of whose name is on the deed. Title companies in Milwaukee will often ask for a spouse's signature even when only one name is on the title, and that comes up in divorces and when a spouse has died.

Foreclosure Goes Through Court, and the Clock Has Options

Every Wisconsin mortgage foreclosure is judicial. The lender sues in circuit court, gets a judgment, and the sheriff sells the house later. Between judgment and sale is the redemption period. For mortgages signed on or after April 27, 2016, it is usually 3 months if the lender gives up a deficiency judgment and 6 months if it does not. Older mortgages use 6 and 12 months.

Here is the detail most owners never hear: if you listed the home with a real estate broker before the judgment and are trying to sell in good faith, you can ask the court to extend a 3-month period to 5 months, or a 6-month period to 8. The court also has to confirm the sheriff's sale at a hearing before the deed passes. Our page on selling before a foreclosure auction covers what to gather first.

Milwaukee Tax Bills and In Rem Foreclosure

Wisconsin taxes are stated per $1,000 of assessed value. A comparison of Milwaukee County municipalities lists the City of Milwaukee's total rate at $20.80 per $1,000 for 2025, down from $22.93 for 2024.

Worked example: a Milwaukee house assessed at $150,000 would owe 150 × $20.80 = $3,120 at that rate, before any state credits on the bill. The City offers an installment plan, but taxes that stay unpaid can end in an in rem foreclosure, where the City Treasurer takes the property itself through a court judgment rather than suing the owner. Owners with back taxes should sell or set up a payment plan long before that stage.

Times a Milwaukee Listing Will Net More

If the house is livable, taxes are current and you can wait 60 to 90 days, a Milwaukee listing agent will usually get a higher price than a cash buyer. That also preserves the extended redemption period if foreclosure is underway. A cash or partnership offer fits better for vacant homes with code orders, rentals with long-term tenants, estates under the affidavit process with heirs scattered across states, or houses facing in rem action.
