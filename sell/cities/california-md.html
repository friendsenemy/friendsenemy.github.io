Selling a California, MD House When Orders, a Rental, or an Estate Forces It

Pax River Orders Set the Calendar

California is an unincorporated community in St. Mary's County, just up the road from Naval Air Station Patuxent River. A large share of homeowners here work on base or for the contractors around it, and that shapes how houses get sold. Permanent change of station orders arrive with a report date, not a flexible closing window, and an owner who bought a few years ago may have to decide quickly whether to sell, rent the house out from another duty station, or try to do both at once.

Many former service members and contractors also kept their California houses as rentals after they left. Years later, some of those landlords are managing a tenant turnover, a worn-out HVAC system, and a property manager from hundreds of miles away.

Three Situations We Hear About Most

Orders with a short fuse

If you have a firm report date and a house that needs paint, carpet, and repairs before it would show well, the time to prepare it may not exist. A cash sale that closes in as few as 7 days for a simple transaction can line up with a move date.

The long-distance landlord

Owners who rented the house out and now want out often face a lease in place, deferred maintenance, and a tenant who may or may not cooperate with showings. Our page for landlords ready to sell a rental explains how a sale can work with a tenant still living there.

An estate in Leonardtown

When an owner dies, the estate goes through the St. Mary's County Register of Wills in Leonardtown, the county seat. If the probate assets are $50,000 or less, or $100,000 or less when a surviving spouse is the only heir, Maryland's small-estate process may apply. Otherwise expect regular administration, with the personal representative signing on the estate's behalf.

The Tax Bill on a California House

There is no town government in California, so no municipal tax is added. The FY2027 rates are St. Mary's County $0.8478 + state $0.112 = $0.9598 per $100 of assessment.

St. Mary's County holds the Homestead Tax Credit cap at 3%, one of the tighter caps in Maryland, meaning the taxable assessment on an owner-occupied principal residence cannot rise more than 3% a year. That protection is tied to living in the house. Once it becomes a rental, or passes to an heir who lives somewhere else, the credit generally ends, and the bill can jump at the next reassessment. Owners who move out under orders should check their status with SDAT.

Is a Cash Buyer Your Best Option?

Often it is not. If your house is in good condition, you have time before your report date, and everyone on the deed agrees, listing with a local agent who knows the base market will usually net you more. Military buyers using VA loans are a steady part of demand in this area, and a well-kept house can do well with them.

A direct sale earns its place when the house would fail a VA or FHA inspection, when you are already gone and cannot manage repairs, when a tenant complicates showings, or when an estate or title problem would stop a financed buyer.
