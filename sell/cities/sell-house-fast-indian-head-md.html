Selling an Indian Head House at the End of Route 210

A Navy town on the Potomac, with its own tax

Indian Head is an incorporated town at the tip of the peninsula where Mattawoman Creek meets the Potomac River in western Charles County, at the end of Indian Head Highway (Route 210). The town grew up around what is now Naval Support Facility Indian Head, and the base still shapes the local housing market. Civilian and military workers come and go on transfers, and a lot of houses in town have been rentals at one point or another.

That history produces a recognizable set of selling problems: owners who received PCS orders and could not sell in time, landlords tired of managing from another state, and older houses left to children who live elsewhere.

Running the numbers on an Indian Head tax bill

Because Indian Head is an incorporated town, owners pay three rates. For FY2027 (July 2026 to June 2027): Charles County at $1.112, the Town of Indian Head at $0.34, and the state at $0.112, for $1.564 per $100 of assessed value. On a house assessed at $240,000, that is about $3,754 a year, or roughly $313 a month that keeps running while a house sits empty.

Charles County's Homestead Tax Credit limits yearly increases in an owner-occupied home's taxable assessment to 10%. Heirs who inherit and do not live in the house generally do not get the credit, and a former owner who moved away on orders and rented the house out may have lost it too.

Waterfront rules and older houses

Much of Indian Head is close to tidal water on the Potomac or Mattawoman Creek, and land within 1,000 feet of tidal water falls under Maryland's Critical Area law. That can limit how much a buyer can expand or rebuild. Combine that with older frame houses that need roofs, windows and systems, and you have properties that retail buyers with financing tend to pass over.

If you are the one leaving on orders

  • Decide early whether you want to rent or sell; trying to do both at the last minute rarely works.
  • Price out what it costs to manage a rental from a new duty station, including vacancies.
  • Read our guide to selling a Maryland house on PCS orders before your report date.

Probate goes to La Plata

Estates for Indian Head property are opened with the Register of Wills for Charles County in La Plata, the county seat, and overseen by the Orphans' Court. If the probate estate is $50,000 or less, or $100,000 or less where a surviving spouse is the only heir, a shorter small-estate process may be available. Otherwise a personal representative is appointed and can sell the house with the court's process in view. This is general information, not legal advice; ask a Maryland attorney about your estate.
