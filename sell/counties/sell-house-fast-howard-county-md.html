Is a Cash Sale Right for Your Howard County House? An Honest Look at Taxes, Estates and Repairs

Start with the honest question: do you even need a cash buyer?

Howard County has some of the strongest home values in Maryland, and buyer demand in places like Columbia, Ellicott City, Clarksville and Elkridge tends to hold up. If your house is in reasonable shape, the people on the deed agree, and you aren't racing a deadline, listing with a local agent will almost certainly net you more than any cash offer, including one from us.

A cash or partnership offer makes sense in Howard County for narrower reasons: a house that hasn't been updated in decades, an estate that is stuck, owners who disagree, or a situation where time and certainty matter more than the last dollar.

One tax rate for the whole county

Unlike most Maryland counties, Howard has no incorporated municipalities. Columbia, despite its size, is an unincorporated planned community, and Ellicott City is not a separate city either. No town tax is layered on, so only the county and state rates apply everywhere. (Columbia properties also carry the Columbia Association annual charge, which is separate from property tax.)

For a house assessed at $520,000, the FY2027 rates (county $1.044 + state $0.112 = $1.156 per $100) come to about $6,011 a year, around $501 a month. On a vacant house during a slow estate, that's a real cost.

The county's Homestead Tax Credit caps the yearly increase in an owner-occupant's taxable assessment at 5%. Once the house passes to an heir who doesn't live there, the credit generally no longer applies.

Estates are handled in Ellicott City

Ellicott City is the county seat, and the Howard County Register of Wills and Orphans' Court are located there. When a homeowner dies, that's where the estate is opened and a personal representative appointed. The PR is the person who signs a sale contract and deed. Maryland's small-estate process covers estates under $50,000, or $100,000 if the surviving spouse is the sole heir, but most Howard County homes are worth more than that on their own, so a regular estate is the usual route.

When an adult child is handling a parent's house while the parent is still alive, the question is a power of attorney rather than probate. Our guide to selling a parent's house with a power of attorney in Maryland explains what to check first. A Maryland attorney can review the documents.

Old Ellicott City and the Patapsco

Historic Ellicott City sits in a narrow valley where the Tiber and Hudson branches meet the Patapsco River. Main Street flooded badly in 2016 and again in 2018, and houses on and near it carry that history into any sale: insurance questions, inspection findings, and buyers who want to know about water. Those stone and frame houses can be beautiful and still be hard to sell to a buyer who needs a conventional loan.

Other situations that come up here

  • Original-condition houses from the 1960s and 70s in Columbia villages, where the kitchen, roof and systems all come due at once.
  • Co-owners after a divorce or a breakup who can't agree on a price. See whether a co-owner can force a sale in Maryland.
  • Rentals near Fort Meade or the I-95 corridor where the landlord is ready to stop.
