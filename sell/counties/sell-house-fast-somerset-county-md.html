Somerset County, MD: Selling Low-Lying, Inherited or Hard-to-Finance Houses

Somerset County is Maryland's southernmost county on the lower Eastern Shore, almost all of it low marsh, tidal creeks and farmland between the Wicomico, Manokin and Pocomoke rivers and Tangier Sound. Princess Anne is the county seat, home to the University of Maryland Eastern Shore, and Crisfield sits at the end of the road to the water. For a seller, the water is the starting point: much of the county falls within 1,000 feet of tidal water, and Maryland's Critical Area law applies there.

Water, flooding and what buyers ask about

In low-lying places like Crisfield, Deal Island and the communities along Tangier Sound, a house's flood exposure and insurance cost can matter as much as its condition. Buyers using a mortgage in a flood zone generally have to carry flood insurance, and that can shrink the pool of buyers. Within the Critical Area, state rules can limit rebuilding footprints, clearing and new hard surface. Many rural Somerset homes also rely on private wells and septic systems, which need to pass inspection for most loans.

Tax rates in Somerset County

Only two Somerset towns levy their own property tax: Crisfield and Princess Anne. Everywhere else, owners pay only the county and state rates. For FY2027 (July 2026 through June 2027) that is $1.000 county plus $0.112 state, or $1.112 per $100 of assessed value.

A house inside Crisfield or Princess Anne adds that town's rate on top.

The county's Homestead Tax Credit caps the annual increase in an owner-occupant's taxable assessment at 10%. Heirs who don't live in the house generally can't claim it.

Estates opened in Princess Anne

The Somerset County Register of Wills and Orphans' Court are in Princess Anne. Because many Somerset houses are modest in value, Maryland's small-estate process, available for estates of $50,000 or less, or $100,000 when the surviving spouse is the only heir, can apply more often here than in the western shore suburbs. That can shorten the path to a sale.

Older family properties sometimes have title that was never cleaned up after earlier deaths, with land passed down for generations without an estate being opened. Those cases need a title review before anyone can sell. Our page on selling a house with liens or title problems explains what that work involves.

When you'll do better listing

If your Somerset house is sound, out of the worst flood risk, the owners agree and there's no deadline, a local agent is likely to get you more than a cash buyer. That's especially true for well-kept homes near Princess Anne.

A cash sale or partnership offer fits when the house needs heavy work, the septic failed, there are back taxes or the property is heading to tax sale, heirs are scattered or disagree, or a co-owner, divorce or incarceration complicates signing. Useful papers to have on hand

  • The most recent tax bill and any delinquency notices.
  • The deed, or the names of everyone you think is on it.
  • Any flood insurance or elevation certificate.
