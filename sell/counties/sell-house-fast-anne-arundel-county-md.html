Selling a House in Anne Arundel County When the Waterfront or an Estate Gets Complicated

So much of this county touches tidal water, and that changes what a house is worth

Anne Arundel County wraps around the Severn, Magothy, South and West Rivers and fronts the Chesapeake Bay for miles. For a seller, that shoreline is more than scenery. Land within 1,000 feet of tidal water falls under Maryland's Critical Area law, which can limit how much of a lot can be covered, whether a tired cottage can be torn down and replaced at a bigger footprint, and what can be built close to the water. When an older waterfront or water-privileged house needs major work, the buyer is really pricing what the county and state will let them do with the lot, not just the house.

That is why two houses a mile apart, one inland near Glen Burnie and one on a creek off the Bay, can sell to very different buyers for very different reasons.

Estates go through the Register of Wills in Annapolis

Annapolis is both the state capital and the county seat, and the Anne Arundel Register of Wills and Orphans' Court sit there. If a parent died owning a house in Pasadena, Severna Park, Crofton or anywhere else in the county, the estate is opened in Annapolis. A personal representative is appointed, and that person is the one who signs a deed once the estate has authority to sell.

  • Estates under $50,000 (or $100,000 when a surviving spouse is the only heir) may qualify for Maryland's small-estate process.
  • When several children inherit and one wants to keep the house, the sale can stall for months. Our guide on what happens when Maryland heirs can't agree to sell walks through the options short of court.
  • For questions about who has the right to sign, a Maryland estate attorney is the right person to ask.

What the house costs to hold in FY2027

Outside the two municipalities that levy their own tax, Annapolis and Highland Beach, an owner pays only the county and state rates. On a house assessed at $410,000, the FY2027 numbers (county $0.968 + state $0.112 = $1.080 per $100) come to about $4,428 a year, or roughly $369 a month. Inside Annapolis or Highland Beach, a town rate is added on top of that.

Anne Arundel's Homestead Tax Credit caps the yearly rise in an owner-occupant's taxable assessment at 2%, one of the tighter caps in Maryland. Long-time owners can be paying tax on an assessment far below market. When the house passes to an heir who does not live in it, that credit generally ends, and the bill can climb toward the full assessed value. Heirs are sometimes surprised by the jump.

Repairs, Fort Meade moves, and other reasons people call

Much of the county's housing was built in waves after the 1950s, and some of it has not had a new roof, panel or septic system in a long time. Our article on selling an Anne Arundel home that needs major repairs covers how repair estimates affect offers. Other sellers are on military orders out of Fort George G. Meade, landlords done with a rental near BWI, or owners behind on taxes before the county's tax sale.

Be honest with yourself about listing first

If the house is in good condition, everyone on the deed agrees, and nothing forces a quick sale, a local listing agent will likely net you more than any cash buyer, including us. Anne Arundel has steady buyer demand, and a clean house there usually does not need a discount to sell.

Where Pages of Purpose fits is the house with a problem attached: an estate that isn't settled, co-owners who disagree, a lien, a code issue, or a waterfront lot where the rebuild math is uncertain. We can make a cash offer, or a partnership offer when that would net you more. A simple cash sale can close in as few as 7 days; an estate or title issue takes as long as it takes to fix properly.

A few questions worth asking before you decide

  1. Is the house inside the Critical Area, and does that limit what a buyer can build?
  2. Has an estate been opened in Annapolis, and who is the personal representative?
  3. Will the homestead credit end when the owner moves out or passes away?
