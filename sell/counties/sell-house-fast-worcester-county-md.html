Selling a Worcester County House or Ocean City Condo: Second Homes, Estates and Town Taxes

A county where many owners don't live here

Worcester County is Maryland's only oceanfront county. Ocean City and the condos and cottages around it draw owners from Baltimore, Washington, Pennsylvania and beyond, and many of them use the place as a vacation home or a seasonal rental. That shapes how sales happen here. A lot of sellers are out of state, the property may be booked by renters for the summer, and the person handling it is sometimes an heir who has never managed a rental.

Inland, the county is different: farmland, Snow Hill and Pocomoke City along the Pocomoke River, Berlin's historic downtown, and rural houses on wells and septic.

Condos and beach houses near the water

Ocean-block condos come with association dues, special assessments for building repairs, and rental programs that a buyer will ask about. Older beach houses and bayside homes sit near tidal water, where Maryland's Critical Area law can limit expansion and rebuilding within 1,000 feet of the shore. Flood zones and insurance costs are part of every conversation. Assateague Island and the coastal bays nearby are protected land, which is one reason buildable waterfront is limited.

Which towns add their own tax

Four municipalities in Worcester levy their own property tax: Berlin, Ocean City, Pocomoke City and Snow Hill. Outside them, owners pay only the county and state rates.

For a house outside town limits assessed at $360,000, the FY2027 rates (county $0.815 + state $0.112 = $0.927 per $100) come to about $3,337 a year, roughly $278 a month. An Ocean City condo or a house in Berlin adds the town rate on top.

Worcester's Homestead Tax Credit cap is listed at 0%, meaning an owner-occupant's taxable assessment does not rise year to year while the credit applies. It only covers a principal residence, though, so a vacation home never had it, and an heir who doesn't live in an inherited house generally doesn't get it either.

Estates are filed in Snow Hill

Snow Hill is the county seat, and the Worcester Register of Wills and Orphans' Court are there. An estate with an Ocean City condo or a farmhouse near Pocomoke is opened in Snow Hill, even if the owner lived and died in another state. In that case there can be an estate in the home state plus an ancillary estate in Maryland for the Worcester property.

Estates under $50,000, or $100,000 when a surviving spouse is the only heir, may use Maryland's small-estate process. A Maryland estate attorney is the right person to sort out out-of-state estates. Our article on heirs who can't agree helps when a family beach house has several owners.

Should you sell to a cash buyer?

For a clean condo or house in good condition, with owners in agreement and no hurry, listing with a local agent who knows the resort market will almost always bring more. That's the straightforward answer.

A cash or partnership offer is worth a look when:

  • the house or unit needs major work, or a large special assessment is coming;
  • it's part of an estate, especially one with heirs in several states;
  • you're a landlord ready to stop managing seasonal tenants (see tired landlords selling Maryland rentals);
  • there are liens or back taxes.
