Selling a Baltimore County House: Towson Estates, One Tax Rate, No Towns

A Baltimore mailing address does not mean Baltimore City

Baltimore County wraps around Baltimore City on three sides, and many homes in places like Catonsville, Dundalk, Parkville and Pikesville carry a "Baltimore, MD" mailing address. They are not in the city. That matters the moment an owner dies: the estate is opened with the Register of Wills for Baltimore County in Towson, the county seat, and the Orphans' Court there handles it. Families sometimes file in the city by mistake and lose weeks. Check the property on the state's SDAT records first; it will list the jurisdiction.

Baltimore County is also unusual in Maryland because it has no incorporated municipalities at all. There is no town or city tax layered on top anywhere in the county. Towson, Essex, Owings Mills and every other community are governed directly by the county.

One rate, worked out on a typical house

For FY2027 (July 2026 to June 2027), the county rate is $1.10 per $100 of assessed value and the state adds $0.112, for $1.212. Because no municipalities levy their own tax, that is the rate everywhere in the county. On a house assessed at $265,000, the annual bill is about $3,212, or roughly $268 a month, and it keeps coming while a house sits empty during an estate.

The county caps yearly increases in an owner-occupant's taxable assessment at 4% through the Homestead Tax Credit. Once a house passes to an heir who lives elsewhere, that credit generally ends, and the taxable value can rise toward the full assessment.

Housing stock and the problems that come with it

Much of the county's older housing is brick rowhouses, duplexes and postwar single-family homes on small lots, especially on the east side near Back River and Middle River and in the inner suburbs along the city line. Common issues we are asked about:

  • Ground rent. Maryland ground leases are best known in the city, but some older county homes carry them too. An unpaid ground rent has to be cleared before closing.
  • Basement water and aging systems in houses that have not been updated since the original owner bought them.
  • Waterfront limits. Homes along the bay shoreline and tidal rivers fall under the Critical Area law, which restricts building within 1,000 feet of tidal water.
  • Tenants in place in rentals that a landlord or an estate wants to sell. See selling a rental property in Maryland.

Is a cash buyer the right call here?

Often it is not. A clean house near Towson University or in a well-kept neighborhood, owned by people who agree, with no clock running, will usually net more on the open market through a local agent, even after commission. Pages of Purpose is worth a call when the house cannot easily go that route: heirs who disagree, a co-owner who will not sign, liens or back taxes, foreclosure, a house with an owner who is incarcerated, or repairs a buyer's lender will not accept.

In those cases Dustin Ray can make a cash offer or a partnership offer, and a simple sale can close in as few as 7 days. When the issue is a family split, our article on heirs who cannot agree on a Maryland house explains what each person can and cannot force.

Small estates in Towson

If the probate assets total $50,000 or less ($100,000 when a surviving spouse is the only heir), Maryland's small-estate process may apply, which is faster. Most houses exceed that, so a personal representative with authority to sell is the usual path. This is general information, not legal advice; a Maryland estate attorney can confirm what applies to your situation.
