Privacy Policy

What Pages of Purpose LLC collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Effective November 4, 2026. Last updated November 7, 2026.

Pages of Purpose LLC (“we,” “us”) is a real estate investment company based in Maryland. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit pagesofpurposellc.com (the “site”), fill out a form, call us, or text with us. By using the site you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the site.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out a form, call, text or email us, you may give us your name, phone number, email address, the address and details of a property, your relationship to it, and anything you choose to write about your situation. Please share only what you are comfortable sharing. Do not send Social Security numbers, bank or card numbers, or medical information through the site.

Information collected automatically. When you visit, our site and the service providers listed in section 3 collect technical information such as your IP address, approximate location (city or region), browser and device type, the pages you view, how long you stay, how you scroll and click, the website that referred you, and any advertising campaign tags in the link you used.

2. How we use information

  • To review your property, answer your questions and, if appropriate, make an offer or introduce an investment partner.
  • To contact you by phone, text or email about your request.
  • To understand which pages help people and to improve the site.
  • To measure our advertising and show our ads to people who have visited the site.
  • To prevent spam and abuse, and to comply with law.

3. Cookies, analytics, session recording and advertising tools

The site uses cookies, pixels and similar technologies provided by the companies below. These companies receive the automatically collected information described in section 1 directly from your browser and handle it under their own privacy policies.

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) measures visits and page views on every page, including clicks on our phone number and form submissions (without what you typed). Google Privacy Policy.
  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation) records how visitors move through our pages (mouse movement, clicks, scrolling) as heat maps and session replays so we can fix confusing pages. Text you type into form fields is masked and is not recorded. Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms, Inc.) tells Meta which pages of our site were visited and when a form was submitted or our phone number was tapped, so we can measure ads and show our ads on Facebook and Instagram to people who visited. We have turned off Meta’s automatic data collection and advanced matching. We do not send Meta your name, phone number, email address, property address or anything you type into our forms. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Formspree receives and delivers form submissions to us by email. Formspree Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses.

Notice and continued use. On your first visit, a notice at the bottom of the page explains these tools. Microsoft Clarity and the Meta Pixel load only after that notice has been shown and you continue using the site, or click “Got it.” If you click “Opt out,” they do not load. Google Analytics loads on every page visit.

Advertising landing pages. On pages built for visitors arriving from our ads, Microsoft Clarity and the Meta Pixel load when the page opens, while the notice is shown, so we can measure whether our ads reach the right people. Clicking “Opt out” on the notice or in “Your privacy choices” stops them, and they never load if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal.

Some of our pages explain difficult situations such as probate, divorce, debts or a family member’s incarceration. Visiting a page does not mean the topic applies to you, and we do not use page visits to draw conclusions about any individual.

4. Your choices and how to opt out

  • “Your privacy choices” link. Use the link at the bottom of every page to turn Microsoft Clarity and the Meta Pixel off (or back on) in that browser.
  • Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, we treat it as an opt-out and Microsoft Clarity and the Meta Pixel do not load.
  • Google Analytics. You can install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Meta. You can manage ads on Facebook and Instagram in your Meta ad preferences.
  • Industry tools. You can also use the Digital Advertising Alliance’s WebChoices page, and your browser settings can block or delete cookies.
  • Marketing messages. Reply STOP to any text, or ask us by phone or email to stop contacting you.

Opting out of these tools does not affect our forms or phone line.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only:

  • With the service providers listed in section 3, which help us run the site, measure it and advertise it.
  • With professionals involved in evaluating or completing a transaction you ask about, such as title companies, closing attorneys, surveyors and contractors.
  • With an investment partner that may purchase or co-purchase your property, only in connection with your request. We will tell you who the buyer is before you sign anything.
  • When required by law, to protect our rights or safety, or in connection with a sale or reorganization of our business.

Under some state laws, letting advertising companies such as Meta collect information on our site to show ads may be considered “sharing” for targeted advertising. You can opt out of that at any time as described in section 4.

6. Your rights, including state privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, and to opt out of targeted advertising. We extend these choices to all U.S. visitors, whether or not a state law requires it. To make a request, email pagesofpurposellc@gmail.com or call (227) 235-9530. We may need to verify your identity, and we will not treat you differently for exercising these rights. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision.

California residents. Under the California Online Privacy Protection Act, we honor Do Not Track signals as described above, and you can visit the site anonymously. If the California Consumer Privacy Act applies to us, the categories of personal information we collect are identifiers, contact details, property information, internet activity and approximate location, used for the purposes in section 2 and shared as described in section 5.

7. How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and transaction records as long as needed to respond to you, complete or document a transaction, and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. Analytics and advertising providers keep data under their own retention settings; for example, Microsoft Clarity keeps recordings for a limited period.

8. Security

The site uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Inquiries are delivered to a limited number of people who need them to help you. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please do not send highly sensitive information through the site.

9. Children

The site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Text messaging (SMS)

If you check the optional text-message box on a form, you agree to receive text messages from Pages of Purpose LLC to schedule appointments, provide updates and answer general inquiries about your property. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent to texts is not a condition of any purchase or offer.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, except service providers who help us deliver messages.

SMS Consent Disclosure: I agree to receive text messages from Pages of Purpose LLC about my property inquiry, including replies to my questions, appointment scheduling and offer updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or sale. See our Privacy Policy and Terms.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be posted on this page.
