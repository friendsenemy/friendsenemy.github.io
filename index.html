How a sale works with us

Simple or complicated, the process is the same three steps. The difference is how long step two takes.

  1. Tell us what’s going on

    Use the short form or call. The basics are enough: where the house is, who owns it, and what’s making it complicated. The address is optional.

  2. 🔍We untangle it and propose a path

    We look into the title, the heirs and any liens, then come back with options: a cash offer, a partnership, or plain guidance on next steps.

  3. 🔑Close on your timeline

    You choose. We handle the paperwork and closing costs. Simple sales close in as few as 7 days; complex ones take as long as the legal process requires.

Questions people ask first

What kinds of situations do you specialize in?

We specialize in complex real estate situations: probate, missing heirs, decades-old liens, broken chains of title, inheritance disputes, and forgotten estates. Our team includes experienced attorneys, private investigators, and genealogical researchers.

Do you only buy houses for cash, or do you offer partnerships?

We offer both. Quick cash solutions when you need to move fast, or partnership arrangements when that fits your situation better. We tailor the path to what works for you.

How fast can you close on my house?

We can close in as few as 7 days when the situation is straightforward. Complex situations involving probate, missing heirs, or title cleanup take longer because we do the work right — but we move as quickly as the legal process allows.

Do I need to make repairs before selling?

Absolutely not. We buy houses in any condition — fire damage, foundation issues, mold, hoarding, major repairs needed, you name it. Sell as-is.

Are there any fees or commissions?

Zero fees, zero commissions. We cover all closing costs. The amount you agree to is the amount you walk away with.

Do you buy properties with title problems?

Yes — that’s one of our specialties. Clouded titles, breaks in chain, old liens, unknown heirs. We have the expertise and resources to resolve title issues and still close the deal.

Is your offer really no-obligation?

100%. There’s no cost and no commitment. If our offer or proposal doesn’t work for you, no hard feelings. We want it to be the right fit for both sides.
