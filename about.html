About the company

Dustin Ray and Pages of Purpose LLC

A Maryland-based cash buyer that specializes in the houses other buyers walk away from: probate, heirs who can't agree, owners in jail, co-owners who won't cooperate, divorces, liens and broken titles. Buying in all 50 states since the company was founded.

Who we are

Pages of Purpose LLC was founded by Dustin Ray after more than a decade spent in property research, title resolution and real estate investing. Most of that time was spent on the unglamorous side of real estate: pulling deeds and probate files, tracing heirs through county records, and figuring out who actually owns a house when the paperwork says three different things.

That work taught us something most cash-buyer companies never learn. The houses that are hardest to sell are usually hard for a legal reason, not a physical one. A roof can be replaced in a week. An estate that was never opened, a brother who refuses to sign, or an owner serving a sentence two states away takes knowledge, patience and a buyer willing to do the work. That is the business we chose.

What we actually do

We buy houses directly from owners, heirs, executors, administrators and attorneys-in-fact, for cash, in any condition, in every state. Specifically, the situations we handle every month:

  • Probate and inherited houses — including estates that haven't been opened yet, and houses still full of a parent's belongings. What happens to a house when someone dies without a will.
  • Heirs or siblings who can't agree — we can buy the whole house from all of you, or just one person's share. Options when one heir refuses to sell.
  • Owners who are incarcerated — power of attorney, facility notaries, and closing without the owner present. How to sell a house while in jail or prison.
  • Co-owner disputes and partition — a cash sale of your interest is usually faster and cheaper than a partition lawsuit. Can a co-owner sell a house?
  • Divorce sales — one clean number, one closing date, no showings. Selling a house during divorce.
  • Liens, back taxes and title problems — paid at settlement out of the purchase price, so you never write the check.

How we work

1. You ask a question — no address required

Every page on this site has a short form. You don't have to give us the address, and you don't have to want an offer. Plenty of people write in to ask whether they even can sell, and we answer that for free.

2. We do the research before we quote a number

We pull the deed, check for an open estate, look for liens and judgments, and figure out who has authority to sign. If a probate case needs to be opened first, we tell you how, and we can wait for it. If the situation is better solved some other way — a family buyout, a quitclaim, keeping the house — we say so.

3. You get a written cash offer, and a real closing date

No financing contingency, no appraisal, no inspection renegotiation. We can buy a whole property or a partial interest. If you accept, we open settlement with a title company licensed in the property's state; if you don't, nothing happens and nobody chases you.

Why this site has so many guides

Because the people we help are usually dealing with a death, a divorce, a family fight or an incarceration at the same time they're dealing with a house, and the information they need is scattered across court websites and lawyer blogs. We write the guides we wish our own sellers had found first. They are general information, not legal advice — the rules differ by state, so check with an attorney licensed where the property sits before you sign anything.
